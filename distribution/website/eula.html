3. The integrity check

Your Pit Box checks at startup that its own launcher code has not been modified. If that check fails, the application refuses to start and shows a message explaining why.

To be explicit about what that does not do: it never deletes, moves, encrypts or damages anything — not the application, not your telemetry, not your files. If the check ever fails on a legitimate copy, which a damaged install or an over-eager antivirus can cause, reinstalling fixes it. Your recorded sessions in PitWallData are never touched.

4. You need your own AI provider account

The spoken radio runs on OpenAI's service, and the engineer's reasoning runs on OpenAI by default — or, if you choose, on another supported provider (Anthropic's Claude, DeepSeek, Kimi / Moonshot AI, or a compatible endpoint you configure) using an API key that you supply. That means:

  • Those calls are billed to your account with that provider — your own OpenAI account by default. Your Pit Box itself is free; I never see, hold or charge that money.
  • Your use of each provider is governed by that provider's own terms, not these.
  • If a provider changes its pricing, models or availability, the features that call it are affected and there is nothing I can do about it. Everything that runs locally — telemetry, strategy, analysis, the dashboard — keeps working regardless.
  • The spoken radio (speech-to-text and the engineer's voice) always uses OpenAI. Without an OpenAI key the engineer answers typed questions using the provider you configured, and stays silent on the radio.

5. What data goes where

Your telemetry stays on your computer. The session database, the raw 60 Hz trace files and your Windows username are never uploaded anywhere.

When you ask a question that genuinely needs judgement, a compact summary of the situation and selected computed results are sent to the AI provider you configured — OpenAI by default, or Claude, DeepSeek, Kimi or your own compatible endpoint if you chose one — so it can answer. With cloud transcription, recorded audio clips are sent to OpenAI for transcription. If you enable the optional Realtime voice mode, microphone audio is streamed to OpenAI while a radio session is active. Nothing is sent to me, and there is no analytics or telemetry reporting back to the developer.

It is not sold, shared or linked to anything the software does. Reply to any of those emails, or write to me, and I will delete it.

If you leave a review on the site, the name and text you enter are published once I have read them, with the month you wrote them; I do not edit reviews. An email address you add with a review is used only to reply to you about it: it is not shown and not added to the update list. To keep spam out, a review is stored with a one-way hash of the connection it came from, which cannot be turned back into an address. Write to me and I will remove a review you posted.
