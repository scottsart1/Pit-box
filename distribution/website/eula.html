5. What data goes where

Your telemetry stays on your computer. The session database, the raw 60 Hz trace files and your Windows username are never uploaded anywhere.

When you ask a question that genuinely needs judgement, a compact summary of the situation and selected computed results are sent to OpenAI so it can answer. With cloud transcription, recorded audio clips are sent to OpenAI for transcription. If you enable the optional Realtime voice mode, microphone audio is streamed to OpenAI while a radio session is active. Nothing is sent to me, and there is no analytics or telemetry reporting back to the developer.

Your activation is recorded on the licence server as a code, a one-way hash identifying the computer, and a timestamp. The hash cannot be reversed to identify your machine, and no name, email or address is stored with it.
