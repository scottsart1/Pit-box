What these signals can tell you

Working driving telemetry requires at least 60 consecutive seconds of fresh, advancing, unpaused driving data. Background app launches alone are not active use. Transfer starts do not confirm successful imports.

Reports are voluntary and self-reported, not billing records. Offline devices can report up to 7 days late. Declined sharing, old versions and removed apps are unobserved. Raw daily reports expire after 90 days, and inactive installation metadata is removed after 90 days without activity. There is no historical app-use backfill and no cross-device person tracking.
