The numbers come from the engine.

On Windows or Android, code computes the telemetry and strategy results. When you ask for model-backed interpretation, the model receives compact context and selected computed results, not your raw recording database.

On your device

Receiving telemetry, recording sessions, calculating race data and reviewing saved laps do not require an AI subscription or an internet connection. Local answers can report gaps, temperatures, damage and strategy status without a model call.

Your recordings stay on your device unless you explicitly export or transfer them. Available analysis depends on the quality and amount of recorded data; missing inputs are not a promise of a complete result.

When you enable AI and voice

Model-backed interpretation uses the provider you configure. Spoken radio uses OpenAI speech services; it needs internet and your own OpenAI API key. Provider usage is billed to you separately.

Cloud transcription sends audio clips to OpenAI. Optional Realtime voice streams microphone audio to OpenAI while a radio session is active. Configure voice on each device and check where its sound plays.

Optional · for people using more than one device

Independent does not mean automatically synced.

Each app works on its own and owns its own library. Pair only when you want to copy saved sessions or forward a live feed. Neither is needed to receive the game directly.

Take your history with you · beta

Pair two devices on the same private network using a QR code or invitation, then choose completed sessions to copy. The receiver checks the archive before importing and skips matching history already present.

This is a manual history transfer, not automatic cloud sync. API keys, microphone permissions and live telemetry are not synced. Stop telemetry on the receiving device before importing.

Telemetry transmission · beta

Optional UDP forwarding sends live game packets to another app or device. It is best-effort: a sent packet does not confirm receipt. Check incoming telemetry on the destination before relying on it during a race.

Forwarding does not copy the saved library. Use trusted destinations on your private network and avoid forwarding loops or duplicate feeds.

Questions

Does it work on PS5 / PC, or only one platform?

PS5 / PC describes where the game runs. Windows / Android describes where the engineer runs. Send the game's F1 2026 UDP feed to either engineer over your private network, or use loopback when the game and Windows app share a PC. Android does not need a Windows host. The receiver uses the same packet parser; packet-format support does not guarantee every router or device setup. Follow the setup guide for your chosen app.

Will it get me banned, or count as cheating?

Your Pit Box reads the game's exported UDP telemetry; it does not send driving inputs or modify game files. Check the rules of any league or event you enter: this is not a guarantee of permission in every competition.

How much will the AI usage actually cost?

Less than most people expect, because the common questions never reach a model. Gaps, tyre temperatures, damage and strategy status are computed locally and cost nothing. What costs money is open-ended conversation and deep strategy reasoning during a long race. You can monitor API usage, use prepaid credits and configure billing alerts on your provider's dashboard — OpenAI's by default; Your Pit Box keeps working without the radio if the key stops.

Can I use Claude, DeepSeek or Kimi instead of OpenAI?

Yes, for the engineer's reasoning. Paste that provider's key on the Connection tab and pick it as the engineer's provider — it applies immediately, no restart. The spoken radio (speech-to-text and the engineer's voice) always uses OpenAI, so without an OpenAI key the engineer answers typed questions on the dashboard and stays silent on the radio. Local OpenAI-compatible servers work too, via the custom endpoint.

What data leaves my device?

Compact situation summaries and selected computed results go to the AI provider you configured when a question needs model judgement. The database and raw trace files are not uploaded to the AI provider. Cloud transcription sends recorded audio clips to OpenAI; optional Realtime voice streams microphone audio to OpenAI while a radio session is active. With beta history transfer, explicitly copying sessions sends their saved history and available traces to your paired device on the local network; it does not create a cloud account or sync your API keys.

Will my laptop and tablet automatically stay in sync?

No. Each device records and stores its own sessions. With beta history transfers, you pair devices and choose which completed sessions to copy. A history transfer does not forward the game's live UDP feed: point the game at the receiving device's current address shown in Connection, or separately configure beta UDP forwarding. Do not start an import while that device is recording.

Do I need to be online?

Not for Your Pit Box itself. There is no activation and no account: telemetry, strategy, analysis and the dashboard all work with no connection. Only the spoken radio and model-backed answers need internet, because those are the parts that call your AI provider.

Is it really free? What is the catch?

Yes, and there is no catch. The download is the complete application, nothing is held back for a paid tier, and nothing expires. The only running cost is your own AI provider usage, billed by that provider to you. If Your Pit Box earns you a position, buy me a coffee — a thank-you, not a purchase.
