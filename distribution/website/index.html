The numbers come from the engine.

On Windows or Android, code computes the telemetry and strategy results. When you ask for model-backed interpretation, the model receives compact context and selected computed results, not your raw recording database.

On your device

Receiving telemetry, recording sessions, calculating race data and reviewing saved laps do not require an AI subscription or an internet connection. Local answers can report gaps, temperatures, damage and strategy status without a model call.

Your recordings stay on your device unless you explicitly export or transfer them. Available analysis depends on the quality and amount of recorded data; missing inputs are not a promise of a complete result.

When you enable AI and voice

Model-backed interpretation uses the provider you configure. Spoken radio uses OpenAI speech services; it needs internet and your own OpenAI API key. Provider usage is billed to you separately.

Cloud transcription sends audio clips to OpenAI. Optional Realtime voice streams microphone audio to OpenAI while a radio session is active. Configure voice on each device and check where its sound plays.

Optional · for people using more than one device

Independent does not mean automatically synced.

Each app works on its own and owns its own library. Pair only when you want to copy saved sessions or forward a live feed. Neither is needed to receive the game directly.

Take your history with you · beta

Pair two devices on the same private network using a QR code or invitation, then choose completed sessions to copy. The receiver checks the archive before importing and skips matching history already present.

This is a manual history transfer, not automatic cloud sync. API keys, microphone permissions and live telemetry are not synced. Stop telemetry on the receiving device before importing.

Telemetry transmission · beta

Optional UDP forwarding sends live game packets to another app or device. It is best-effort: a sent packet does not confirm receipt. Check incoming telemetry on the destination before relying on it during a race.

Forwarding does not copy the saved library. Use trusted destinations on your private network and avoid forwarding loops or duplicate feeds.

Set up beta telemetry forwarding

A full send bar is not an import receipt. Very large libraries can still hit archive limits or timeouts: start with small batches and keep the originals until the receiving Library is checked. Previously pruned samples cannot be recovered by transferring a session.

Questions

Does it work on PS5 / PC, or only one platform?

The game runs on PS5 or PC; the engineer runs on Windows or Android. Send the game's F1 2026 UDP feed to either app over your private network, or use loopback when the game and Windows app share a PC. Router and device setups vary, so follow the setup guide for your app.

Will it get me banned, or count as cheating?

It only reads the telemetry the game exports. It never sends inputs or modifies game files. Check the rules of any league you enter.

How much will the AI usage actually cost?

Usually little: gaps, temperatures, damage and strategy status are computed locally for free. Open-ended conversation and deep strategy reasoning cost money on your provider account, so use prepaid credits or billing alerts. The app keeps working without the radio if the key stops.

Can I use Claude, DeepSeek or Kimi instead of OpenAI?

Yes, for reasoning: paste the key on Connection and choose the provider. The spoken radio always uses OpenAI, so without an OpenAI key the engineer answers typed questions only. Local OpenAI-compatible servers work through the custom endpoint.

What data leaves my device?

When a question needs a model, compact summaries and selected results go to your chosen provider; your database and raw traces are not uploaded. Cloud transcription sends audio clips to OpenAI, and optional Realtime voice streams microphone audio while a radio session is active. Beta history transfer copies only the sessions you choose to your paired device on your local network.

Will my laptop and tablet automatically stay in sync?

No. Each device keeps its own sessions. With beta history transfer you pair devices and choose completed sessions to copy. It does not forward the live feed; point the game at the device that should receive it. Do not import while that device is recording.

Do I need to be online?

Not for the app: no activation and no account. Telemetry, strategy, analysis and the dashboard work offline. Only the spoken radio and model-backed answers need internet.

Is it really free? What is the catch?

No catch. The download is the complete app and nothing expires. Your only cost is your own AI provider usage. If it earns you a position, buy me a coffee — a thank-you, not a purchase.
