What it does

Your Pit Box reads all 24 cars from every packet, not just yours, so a question about a rival is answered with that rival's data.

Strategy that plays the result

Plans are ranked by projected finishing position and championship points, not just elapsed time. Undercut maths accounts for cold-tyre out-laps, and it will not recommend a plan that finishes the race disqualified.

Radio you can talk to

Ask for the gap, a rival's last two laps, or what changes the call. Say the wake word or use a button. It can be interrupted mid-sentence, and it remembers standing instructions across sessions.

Calls you didn't ask for

Rival pit stops as they happen, a car behind closing over a rolling window, safety-car delta breaches, damage, and power-unit wear heading for a grid penalty.

Corner coaching with numbers

Not "brake later" but "brake 8 metres later while protecting apex speed" — measured against your own personal best, with differences below the noise floor left unsaid.

Sessions you can reopen

Recorded sessions replay without the game running. Compare two laps distance-aligned, see where the time actually went, and keep a permanent history of every stint.

A connection screen that tells the truth

It separates "the socket is open" from "real telemetry is arriving", recommends the right LAN address for your PS5 / PC, and names the actual bind conflict when something else owns the port.

The part that matters

Most "AI" tools hand your telemetry to a language model and hope. Your Pit Box does the opposite: deterministic code computes every number, and the model is only allowed to say them out loud.

That split is the whole design. A model that invents a lap time sounds exactly as confident as one that read it, and mid-race you cannot tell the difference. So the model never sees raw telemetry to interpret — it receives computed results from an allow-listed set of tools and narrates them.

Common questions never reach a model at all. Gaps, tyre temperatures, damage and strategy status are answered locally, which is why they come back instantly and cost nothing.

Your data stays on your PC. The database, the raw 60 Hz traces and your Windows username are never uploaded. Only compact situation summaries go out, and only for questions that genuinely need judgement.

Questions

Does it work on PS5 / PC, or only one platform?

Both. Your Pit Box's telemetry receiver has no platform-specific code — it reads standard F1 2026 UDP packets the same way regardless of whether they come from a PS5 or the PC version of the game, and licensing is tied to your Windows PC, not to a console. The setup guide covers both: PS5 / PC on your home network, or the game and Your Pit Box sharing one PC. This is based on the packet format and receiver code rather than a dedicated PC hardware test; if you hit anything unexpected, email me and I will help sort it out.

Will it get me banned, or count as cheating?

No. It reads the telemetry stream the game is designed to broadcast, the same one used by overlays, sim dashboards and motion rigs. It never sends inputs to the game and never touches its files.

How much will the OpenAI usage actually cost?

Less than most people expect, because the common questions never reach a model. Gaps, tyre temperatures, damage and strategy status are computed locally and cost nothing. What costs money is open-ended conversation and deep strategy reasoning during a long race. You can monitor API usage, use prepaid credits and configure billing alerts on the OpenAI dashboard; Your Pit Box keeps working without the radio if the key stops.

What data leaves my computer?

Compact situation summaries and selected computed results, only for questions that need model judgement, and only to OpenAI. The database, the raw 60 Hz trace files and your Windows username are never uploaded. Cloud transcription sends recorded audio clips to OpenAI; optional Realtime voice streams microphone audio to OpenAI while a radio session is active.

Do I need to be online?

Once, to activate. After that the app runs offline — telemetry, strategy, analysis and the dashboard all work with no connection. Only the spoken radio needs internet, because that is the part that calls OpenAI.

What happens if I reinstall Windows or change my hardware?

Reinstalling Your Pit Box on the same Windows install is fine. A fresh Windows install or a new PC will need a new activation — email me and I will issue one. I would rather do that by hand than build a system that gets it wrong.
