Each local driver profile has a random ID, and every successful sign-in is reported with it, so one row follows the same profile over time. Sign-ins made offline are queued and appear after the device reconnects; retries are counted once. A profile on another device, or after a reinstall, has a different ID. Times come from the device clock; Received is when the report arrived. Counts do not prove unique people or gameplay. Email addresses, PINs, driver names and API keys are never included.

Downloads, email signups and usage are separate signals, not a linked customer funnel. One person can have several installations. Usage excludes people who declined sharing and older builds; no historical app use can be reconstructed. Offline reports can arrive up to 7 days late. Transfers count starts, not completed imports.
