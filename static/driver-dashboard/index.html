Make it yours

Your screen. Your dashboard.

Auto fits the available screen. Fold or unfold your phone, rotate a tablet, or resize your window. The profile uses browser pixels, so browser zoom and split screen are respected. It does not need to identify your exact device.

Take control. Choose a Galaxy Fold, Galaxy Tab, iPad, laptop or monitor profile. Pick any of the six layouts; your choice stays saved until you select Auto layout. Customize number size, units and cards.

During a race. Open the Driver Dashboard tab in Your Pit Box for live telemetry. Race mode hides setup controls and requests a screen wake lock where supported. The app must keep receiving simulator data. Flags take priority; gaps are race gaps, not physical on-track distance.

Phone installation. In Samsung Internet or Chrome, use the browser menu to add this page to your home screen. On iPad, use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. The public page and offline download use labelled sample data. Live telemetry is available inside the racing app.

Design references. Inspired by the glanceable instruments in SimHub and the relative timing in SIMRacingApps.
