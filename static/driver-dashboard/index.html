During a race. Open the Driver Dashboard tab in Your Pit Box for live telemetry. Race mode hides setup controls and requests a screen wake lock where supported. The app must keep receiving simulator data. Flags take priority; gaps are race gaps, not physical on-track distance.

Phone installation. In Samsung Internet or Chrome, use the browser menu to add this page to your home screen. On iPad, use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. The public page and offline download use labelled sample data. Live telemetry is available inside the racing app.
